About Me

I'm an applied LLM and data scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Groningen (2025) and two years as an applied scientist at Amsterdam UMC's Translational AI Lab. I now work independently on applied GenAI systems. I build LLM-powered and foundation-model systems that solve concrete problems: from domain-adapting an 8B-parameter LLM (Qwen3-8B) with continued pretraining and QLoRA, to retrieval-augmented generation and multi-agent systems, to transformer-based foundation models trained on 1.2M+ clinical records.

I work across the full applied ML stack: pre-training and fine-tuning models on GPU clusters (H100, Slurm), building hybrid-retrieval RAG APIs with reranking and grounded generation, orchestrating LLM agents with Google ADK and LangGraph, and designing the evaluation frameworks and MLOps workflows that take models from prototype to production. Earlier work includes an embedding-based federated learning pipeline deployed at Amsterdam UMC and NHS Blood and Transplant with the University of Cambridge, and GPT-CEP, an LLM pipeline that generates and refines event-processing rules over Kafka/Flink streams.

My PhD and postdoctoral training gave me the depth to design and evaluate these systems rigorously; my focus now is applying that depth to build production-grade LLM and ML products. I've also collaborated with the University of Amsterdam, University of Oslo, TU Ilmenau, and the University of Cambridge.

Projects

LLM Fine-tuning

Commerce Foundation Model

End-to-end domain adaptation of Qwen3-8B on Amazon Reviews 2023 (131.6M interactions processed): continued pretraining with 4-bit QLoRA on a 1M-document / 108M-token corpus, then LoRA fine-tuning (r = 8/16/32) for rating prediction on H100 GPUs via Slurm. Continued pretraining cut held-out perplexity by 44% (19.5 → 10.9); the evaluation honestly reports where downstream gains did and did not follow.
