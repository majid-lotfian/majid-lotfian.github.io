About Me

I'm an applied LLM and data scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Groningen (2024) and postdoctoral experience at Amsterdam UMC's Translational AI Lab. I build and ship LLM-powered and foundation-model systems that solve concrete problems, from GPT-CEP, an applied LLM pipeline that automatically generates and refines event-processing rules from data patterns, to transformer-based foundation models trained on 1.2M+ clinical records.

I work across the full applied ML stack: pre-training and fine-tuning foundation models, designing evaluation and embedding frameworks, and building the data pipelines and MLOps workflows that take models from prototype to production. Recent work includes federated, embedding-based prediction systems built with the University of Cambridge, and LLM-assisted rule generation over high-volume streaming data with Apache Kafka and Flink.

My PhD and postdoctoral training gave me the depth to design and evaluate these systems rigorously; my focus now is applying that depth to build production-grade LLM and ML products. I hold research affiliations with the University of Amsterdam, University of Oslo, and TU Ilmenau.

Projects

Applied LLM

GPT-CEP

Applied LLM system for automated complex event processing rule mining: an LLM reasons over latent data patterns and generates interpretable detection rules, combined with rule-based backends over live Kafka/Flink pipelines with monitoring and feedback loops.

PythonLLMApache KafkaApache FlinkGPT API

FBC-Transformer

Transformer-based foundation model trained from scratch on 1.2M+ clinical records. Custom tokenizer, pre-training loop with masked objectives, multi-task fine-tuning heads, and full evaluation harness with W&B tracking.

PythonPyTorchHuggingFaceW&B

MassSpectra Foundation Model

Transformer-based foundation model for high-dimensional mass spectrometry signals. Pre-training on unlabelled spectra, fine-tuning for classification and retrieval. Custom positional encodings for variable-length spectral inputs.

PythonPyTorchHuggingFace

Embedding Space Discovery

Geometry-based framework for analysing learned embedding spaces from foundation models. Clustering, label-free correspondence metrics, nearest-neighbour retrieval, and visualisation pipelines for discovering latent clinical structure.

PythonPyTorchscikit-learn

APP-CEP

Adaptive pattern-level privacy protection in complex event processing systems. Enforces privacy at the pattern level (not data level) using CEP-like processing in Apache Flink. Published in Elsevier Information Systems (2024).

JavaApache FlinkPrivacy

SmartScreening-CH

Clinical foundation models and ML pipelines for early detection of Congenital Hypothyroidism. Combines deep learning with classical models to support newborn screening; embedding-based patient similarity and classification.
